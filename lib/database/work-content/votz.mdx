My client was having a problem: her volleyball club organize matches 3 days in the week, and every day she needs to ask chatgpt to form balanced teams based on the players's skill level and type all the players's name and skill level (using her cellphone).

I solved her problem by developing a web app, with PWA funcionality so it can run as an installed mobile app, where she can save all the players just once and use every time, and form balanced teams based on the players's skill level with just one click. Also with a buttom to shuffle.
